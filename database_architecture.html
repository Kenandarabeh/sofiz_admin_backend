PILLAR 1 Blockchain & Ledger Segregation

Managed by StellarWallet and WalletSweepLog.

  • Role Segregation: Collection wallets strictly accept retail incoming DZT; Profit wallets lock accumulated net profits; Withdrawal wallets hold operational cash floats.
  • Multi-Tenant Families: GAME, FACTURE, and ESIM never cross-contaminate.
  • Cryptographic Audit: Every balance transfer records an immutable Stellar tx hash with row-level locks (select_for_update).
PILLAR 2 Postal Banking & Glitch Quarantine

Managed by BaridiMobAccount with institutional resilience.

  • Real-World Integration: Synchronizes live BaridiMob accounts (Salah Gaming, Sihaam31).
  • Anomaly Quarantine: When Algérie Poste servers glitch (e.g. reporting erroneous negative amounts), the account is quarantined and safely falls back to last_stable_balance, preserving the Golden Balance.
  • Automated 50 DZD Logging: Transfers automatically record postal fees in ExpenseRecord.
PILLAR 3 Crypto Bridge & Wholesale Sourcing

Managed by BinanceAccount and SupplierAccount (G2Bulk).

  • The Currency Bridge: Solves the time-lag between Algerian Dinar and USD. Funds converted to USDT on Binance remain fully counted as company assets.
  • Dynamic FX Valuation: All foreign currency balances (USD at G2Bulk, USDT at Binance) are continuously converted to DZT via live benchmark exchange rate.
  • Floor Alerts: Alerts if G2Bulk drops below $50.00 USD.
PILLAR 4 3-Stage Outflow & Anti-Double-Spending

Managed by WithdrawalRecord and BaridiMobFundingReceipt.

  • SHA256 File Hash: Uploaded receipts are hashed. Re-uploading an identical receipt is mathematically rejected.
  • Instant Deduction: Uploading a receipt instantly checks and deducts source withdrawal wallet balance; overdrafts are rejected with HTTP 400.
  • 3-Stage Tracking: Tracks progress from Postal transfer (Allocated) -> P2P purchase -> G2Bulk Wholesale credit.

🔍 Systems Architect Review: Why This Flow is Mathematically Closed

Notice the closed-loop nature of the architecture: No money exits without a corresponding counterparty asset entering. When funds move from W2 to BaridiMob, liquidity moves from Stellar to Postal Cash. When Postal Cash buys USDT on Binance, liquidity moves from DZD to USDT. When USDT is transferred to G2Bulk, liquidity becomes wholesale inventory. And when inventory is sold, retail DZT flows back into W3/W11. The Golden Balance equation continuously balances all 5 pillars against original capital plus net realized profits.
